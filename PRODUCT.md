# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel. Specified by the user's brief.

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters outside Unilever, arriving from LinkedIn. They scan fast, distrust adjectives and check that numbers make sense. Within 30 seconds they should know what Control Quest 2026 was, how big it was, and what Ishraq Nasim did.

## Product Purpose

One self-contained portfolio section showcasing Control Quest 2026, Unilever Bangladesh's internal controls awareness programme (Corporate Office edition), and Ishraq Nasim's part in organising it. It will later be lifted into a larger personal site.

## Positioning

The event carries the page; Ishraq's role is brief and factual. Tone: classy, calm, confident. Not flashy, salesy or self-congratulatory. Reads as an annual-report microsite or editorial case study.

## Operating Context

A five-day, detective-themed learning week ("The Road to Green"): daily sessions, daily quizzes with week-long scores, top 15 to a team finale with five detective agencies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No footer, contact or social links.
- No dates or month: Day 1 to Day 5 only.
- Corporate Office edition figures only; never the Kalurghat Factory (KGF) edition.
- No prize pool or money amounts.
- Day names and topic names are locked to the official agenda, word for word.
- All titles in Title Case; every label and title carries an inline lucide icon.

## Brand Commitments

Control Quest logo (fedora, shield, road, checkered flags, "THE ROAD TO GREEN") and the green road-to-city background, both supplied by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/control-quest/logo.webp` (transparent), `public/control-quest/bg.webp`.
- Figures, copy and agenda as given in the brief.
- No event photos yet; `public/control-quest/photos/` will receive them later.
- Must not fabricate: statistics, testimonials, outcomes (outcomes are intended, not measured), quotes, stock photos of Unilever, its offices or people.

## Product Principles

1. Facts first, adjectives never.
2. Text-light: short action phrases, every fact kept.
3. Scale and ownership readable in one scan.
4. A fresher's role stated plainly, never inflated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, reduced-motion respected, works with a thumb on a phone.
